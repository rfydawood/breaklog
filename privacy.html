Privacy policy

BreakLog is a class break and attendance tracker for hifz teachers. This page explains, in plain words, what the app keeps, where, and how to delete it.

Effective 27 October 2026

Who runs the app

BreakLog is made and run by Rafaye Dawood. For any question about your data, or to ask for it to be deleted, email rfy.dawood@gmail.com.

This policy covers the website, the Android app and the Windows app, and the website when it is added to the home screen of an iPhone or iPad. They are all the same app and keep data in the same place. BreakLog used to be called Hifz Class Tracker; nothing about your data changed with the name.

What the app stores

Your Google account. To use the app you sign in with Google. Google then shares your email address, your name and a user ID (a random code that identifies your account). Google may also pass on a link to your profile picture; the app does not use or show it. The app never sees your Google password.

The classes you set up. Everything you type or tap in the app is saved so it is there on all your devices:

  • class names, and the name you give yourself as the teacher;
  • student names, the order of the class list, and whether a student is still in the class;
  • attendance for each day (present, late, absent, left early, with the time and a short note);
  • breaks: which student went, the type (washroom, water, wudhu, other or assigned), when they left and came back, and whether they went over the limit;
  • class times, schedules (the usual day, weekdays that differ, one-off dates and their notes), whole-class breaks, break limits and other settings.
  • which version of the Terms of use and this Privacy policy you accepted, and when.

The app does not ask for, and has no place to store, students’ ages, photos, contact details, addresses, grades or anything else about them.

What stays only on your device

Some things are kept only on the device you are using and are never sent anywhere:

  • the teacher mode PIN, stored only as a scrambled code (a salted hash), never as the number itself, and whether teacher mode is unlocked;
  • which class you had open, and a copy of it so the app opens quickly and works without internet;
  • small preferences, such as whether the tab bar is tucked away;
  • changes made while offline, waiting to be sent when the internet comes back.

Clearing the app’s storage, or uninstalling it, removes these from that device.

Where it is kept

Your account and class data are kept in Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Cloud Firestore for the data), a service run by Google. Google stores it on its servers and acts as a service provider for this app. You can read Firebase’s privacy and security information.

The app and this website are hosted on GitHub Pages, and they load their fonts from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services see the basic details your device sends when it downloads a page or a font (such as its internet address). The app sends them none of your class data.

Why it is kept

Only to run the app: to show your classes, work out the reports, and keep your classes the same on every device you sign in on. Nothing else.

No ads, no tracking, no selling

  • There are no adverts in the app.
  • There is no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting software in the app. (Google’s Firebase Analytics is not included and is switched off.) This website has none either.
  • Your data is never sold, rented or used for marketing.
  • It is not shared with anyone, except Google in its role as the service that stores it, or if the law requires it.

Children and students

The app is made for teachers. Students never use it to sign in and do not have accounts. In class, a student may tap their own tile on the teacher’s tablet to start or end a break, but they cannot see reports or change settings while teacher mode is locked.

Student names are typed in by the teacher. Teachers should only add students they have permission to record, following their school’s and parents’ wishes. You can use first names or initials if you prefer. A parent or school that wants a student’s records removed can ask the teacher, or email rfy.dawood@gmail.com.

Keeping it safe

  • Everything sent between the app and Google travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Access rules on the database let only the signed-in members of a class read or change it. Nobody else can see your classes, even if they know the class’s code.
  • Teacher mode adds a PIN on the tablet so students can’t change records.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Your data stays until you delete it. Removing a student from the class list keeps their past records in your reports; deleting an archived class (Profile → My classes → Show archived → Delete forever) removes that class and all its records.

To delete everything, your account and all your classes:

  • in the app, go to Profile → Delete my account; or
  • on the web, use the delete your account page; or
  • email rfy.dawood@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in with, and it will be done for you.

Once deleted, data is removed from the live database straight away and cannot be brought back. Google may take a short while longer to clear it from its own systems. More about deleting your account.

Backups you download

Profile → Download a backup, and Reports → Export CSV, save a file onto your own device. That file is yours: it is not sent anywhere, and deleting your account does not delete it. Keep it somewhere safe, and delete it when you no longer need it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a change makes a real difference to how your data is used, it will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
