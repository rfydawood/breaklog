Installing

Straight from the website (any device)

Open the app and sign in. To add an icon:

  • Android (Chrome): ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone or iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.

The Android app

Invite only for now. Email rfy.dawood@gmail.com to try it. Google Play is coming later.

On a Windows computer

Get it from the download page. If Windows shows a blue warning, click More info → Run anyway. It’s safe; the app is just new.

Using the app

Signing in

Tap Sign in with Google. Use the same account on every device and your classes stay in step. Your account is on the Profile tab.

More than one class

Profile → My classes → + Add a class. Tap Open to switch. Archive old classes from the “…” menu.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Changes are saved on the device and sent when you’re back online. Keep the app open until it reconnects.

Teacher mode and the PIN

Tap the padlock and set a 4-number PIN. When locked, students can still log breaks, but settings and records need the PIN. Each device has its own PIN.

Forgot the PIN?

Tap the padlock → Forgot the PIN? and sign in again. Your class isn’t changed.

Backups

Your classes are saved online already. For an extra copy: Profile → Download a backup. For a spreadsheet: Reports → Export CSV.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete my account deletes everything for good. You can also use the delete your account page or email rfy.dawood@gmail.com.

To delete one class, archive it, then Show archived → Delete forever.

Reporting a problem

Email rfy.dawood@gmail.com and say:

  • what went wrong;
  • your device;
  • the version number (on the Profile tab).

A screenshot helps. Please don’t send student names.
