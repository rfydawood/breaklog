Help and support

BreakLog is a free class break and attendance tracker for hifz teachers. It keeps track of who is in class, who is out on a break and for how long, and turns that into simple reports. It works on tablets, phones and computers, and keeps your classes the same on all of them.

Installing

Straight from the website (any device)

Open the app in your browser and sign in. That’s all you need. To give it its own icon:

  • Android, in Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu and choose Add to Home screen (or Install app).
  • iPhone or iPad, in Safari: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up), choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

It now opens from its own icon, like any other app, and always has the newest version.

The Android app

There is also an Android app. For now it is shared with testers through the download page; it will come to Google Play later. If you’d like to try it, email rfy.dawood@gmail.com.

On a Windows computer

Go to the download page and choose Windows. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, click More info, then Run anyway. This shows because the app is new, not because anything is wrong.

Using the app

Signing in and keeping devices in step

Tap Sign in with Google. Sign in with the same Google account on every device you use, and your classes appear on each one. A change made on one device shows on the others within a few seconds.

You can see which account you are using under the Profile tab (the circle with your initials).

More than one class

Go to Profile → My classes and tap + Add a class. Each class has its own students, times and limits. Tap Open to switch. A class you no longer teach can be archived from its “…” menu and brought back later with Restore.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once you have signed in and opened your class, you can take attendance and log breaks with no internet. Your changes are kept on the device and sent automatically when it is back online. Keep the app open on the device that has the changes until it reconnects.

Teacher mode and the PIN

Teacher mode stops students changing things on the class tablet. Tap the padlock at the end of the tab bar and choose a 4-number PIN. While it is locked, students can still start and end their own breaks, but settings, the class list, your profile and changes to records ask for the PIN. Reports stay open.

Each device has its own PIN, kept only on that device. Tap the padlock and type the PIN to unlock; tap it again to lock.

Forgot the PIN?

Tap the padlock, then Forgot the PIN? Sign in again with the Google account the class belongs to, and the PIN is cleared so you can set a new one. Nothing in your class is changed.

Backups

Your classes are already saved online in your Google account. For an extra copy, go to Profile → Download a backup. This saves a file with every class onto your device. Keep it somewhere safe. You can also download a spreadsheet of a report with Reports → Export CSV.

Deleting your account and data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your account and all your classes, students and records for good. You can also use the delete your account page on the web, or email rfy.dawood@gmail.com from the Google account you use and it will be done for you.

To delete just one class, archive it first (Profile → My classes → “…” → Archive), then choose Show archived → Delete forever. See the privacy policy for what is kept and for how long.

Organizations

Setting up an organization, with programs and invited teachers, is Coming soon. Until then, each teacher signs in with their own Google account and sets up their own classes.

Reporting a problem

Email rfy.dawood@gmail.com and say:

  • what you were doing and what went wrong;
  • which device you use (for example, an Android tablet, iPhone or Windows computer);
  • the version number, shown on the Profile tab.

A screenshot helps a lot. Please don’t send student names or a backup file unless you are asked to.
