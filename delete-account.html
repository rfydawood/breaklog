Delete your BreakLog account

You can delete your BreakLog account at any time. This removes your account and every class you set up, with all their students, attendance and breaks.

Delete my accountOpens BreakLog’s deletion page

Three ways to do it

  1. In the app: open BreakLog, go to Profile (the circle with your initials), then Delete my account.
  2. On the web: use BreakLog’s deletion page and follow the steps there.
  3. By email: write to rfy.dawood@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in with, and it will be done for you.

What gets deleted

  • your sign-in record in BreakLog (your name, email address and user ID);
  • every class you set up, with its students, attendance, breaks, schedules and settings.

It is removed from the live database straight away and cannot be brought back. Google may take a short while longer to clear it from its own systems.

What isn’t deleted

  • Files you downloaded, such as a backup or a CSV report. They are on your own device; delete them yourself if you no longer need them.
  • Your Google account itself. BreakLog only removes its own data; your Google account is not touched.
  • Things kept only on a device (like the teacher mode PIN) go when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it.

Only want to remove one class?

Archive it first (Profile → My classes → “…” → Archive), then choose Show archived → Delete forever. Your account and other classes stay as they are. Want a copy first? Use Profile → Download a backup.
