Questions

What people ask first

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.

Where is the data kept?

In Google’s cloud (Firebase), under your own Google account. Only you can open it when you sign in. Privacy policy

Do students need accounts?

No. Students are just names on the teacher’s list. Only teachers sign in.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. It keeps logging and catches up when the connection comes back.

Which devices?

Any tablet, phone or computer with a web browser, plus an Android app and a Windows app. On iPhone or iPad, add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

Can I use it on more than one device?

Yes. Sign in with the same Google account on each one and your classes stay in step within seconds.
