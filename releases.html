Release notes

What's new

The newest version is at the top. The website updates itself; the Android and Windows apps update when you open them. Your version number is on the Profile tab.

Version 4.2.1

28 October 2026

  • A new app icon: clock hands inside the BreakLog ring.

Version 4.2

28 October 2026

  • The app is now called BreakLog, with a new icon.
  • Delete your account and all its data from Profile.
  • Accept the Terms and Privacy Policy when you sign in.
  • Windows app 1.0.2: the new icon and name.

Version 4.1

21 October 2026

  • Teacher mode: lock the class tablet with a PIN using the padlock on the tab bar.
  • While locked, students can only start and end their own breaks.
  • New Windows app: your classes in their own window, with Google sign-in through your browser.
  • Windows app 1.0.1: Google sign-in no longer cancels on its own.

Version 4.0.1

7 October 2026

  • Typing works with the tablet turned sideways.
  • Delete archived classes for good.
  • Pick any number of minutes for break limits.
  • Tiles fit on the screen on Wednesdays.

Version 4.0

5 October 2026

  • More than one class: add classes and switch between them from Profile.
  • Each day can have its own class times and breaks.
  • Fixes to Reports and the Roster.

Version 3.8

4 October 2026

  • Tabs along the bottom (Log, Reports, Roster, Settings) that tuck away.
  • New charts and numbers in Reports.
  • A new device now shows the sign-in screen first.

Version 3.7

27 September 2026

  • Sign in with Google to keep your class on your account.
  • Resuming a class keeps the day's start time.
  • Download a backup from the website.

Version 3.6

23 September 2026

  • Set the minutes for every break with a simple stepper.
  • Fixed the over-limit flags.
  • Reports fit the tablet screen.
  • Reset day asks before it clears anything.

First Android app

15 September 2026

  • The app can be installed on an Android tablet.
